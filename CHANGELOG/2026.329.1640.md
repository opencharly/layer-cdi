# 2026.329.1640 — docs(cdi): consumer classes, an NVML probe, and the volume/XDG/FUSE contract

## Summary

The authored `cdi` skill is the contract every GPU image and harness reads, and it was wrong in
several load-bearing places:

- "a CDI device and a userns mapping are mutually exclusive" — false since the derived devices-only
  spec + read-only library binds made keep-id the shape;
- "every GPU image composes both `layer-cdi` and `layer-cuda`" — false for most deployed pods;
- "`pacman -S cuda` depends on `nvidia-utils`" — measured: the closure is `cccl`, `opencl-nvidia`,
  `python`;
- "delete the whole `nvidia*` binary set" — a symptom of a driver-pulling package, not a rule;
- the runtime probe used `nvidia-smi`, which the derived spec does not inject at all.

This PR makes the skill describe the measured contract:

- **three consumer classes** (bundled runtime / links the toolkit / compiles), with the measured
  examples and what each composes — the separation between CUDA-as-a-wheel and CUDA-as-a-system
  package;
- **prevention instead of removal** (no post-install strip; `NoExtract` measured and refuted);
- the **runtime probe from NVML** (`create_string_buffer(80)`), because `nvidia-smi` is absent under
  keep-id;
- corrected **package facts** (`opencl-nvidia` is the whole driver contamination);
- the **FUSE/encrypted-volume contract**: `-allow_other` is required for the host-bind shape (and
  `-allow_root` is not a flag), the in-pod shape avoids it at the cost of `CAP_SYS_ADMIN`, and `0700`
  on the plaintext root is the security control;
- the **bind/XDG contract**, measured with the real Immich stack, including the missing
  `/home/user/.cache`, readiness only after the DB migration, and the old-shape subuid trap.
